## Design Context

### Users
AI-inbox is a desktop workspace for people who collect notes, links, tasks, and AI-generated research during daily knowledge work. They use it while reading, thinking, and triaging incoming material, so the interface should stay calm, scannable, and trustworthy during repeated use.

### Brand Personality
Warm, editorial, and focused. The product should feel like a private reading desk with useful automation nearby: quiet enough for long sessions, polished enough to feel cared for, and direct enough that the user can move from capture to review without ceremony.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a refined light-first desktop app aesthetic with warm paper-like surfaces, restrained amber/terracotta accents, precise borders, compact controls, and editorial typography. Avoid generic AI gradients, loud neon, marketing-page composition, and decorative card overload. Dense operational views should remain functional and scan-friendly.

### Design Principles
- Keep the first screen usable as the product workspace, not a landing page.
- Prefer warm tinted neutrals, crisp hierarchy, and small moments of depth over decorative effects.
- Treat cards, panels, and controls as a small shared system: consistent radius, elevation, borders, and focus states.
- Preserve the V2 data model boundary: visual polish must not reintroduce Markdown/frontmatter app state.
- Favor compact desktop ergonomics with enough breathing room for long reading and triage sessions.
